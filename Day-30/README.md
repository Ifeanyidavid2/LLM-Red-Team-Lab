## Professional Claim Discipline



Portfolio claims are classified conservatively.



**VERIFIED** means the supporting artifact has completed evidence review.



**PRESENT** means the supporting artifact exists but has not yet completed the same level of evidence verification.



Laboratory-based assessment work is not represented as unauthorized testing or production-client exploitation.
