=== Security Interpretation ===
1. Hardening reduced accepted judge manipulation in the repeated qualified benchmark.
2. That improvement relied heavily on escalation rather than automatic correct resistance.
3. Deterministic protected-value detection remained reliable once the policy configuration mismatch was repaired.
4. Full holdout performance did not improve overall; operational accuracy fell from 55% to 40%.
5. Task and refusal recall improved, but attack recall regressed from 20% to 0%.
6. Hardening therefore changed the error distribution rather than eliminating evaluator error.
7. The final architecture is safer against the tested evaluator injection pattern but is not yet a superior general-purpose security evaluator.
=== Final Day 17 Conclusion ===
Judge hardening should be evaluated as a multi-objective problem: manipulation resistance, ordinary accuracy, signal recall, escalation burden, and deterministic invariant preservation must all be measured together.
